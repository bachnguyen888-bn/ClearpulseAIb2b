The clinical gate

Medical questions never get an automatic answer.

If a message mentions swelling, pain, a reaction, or how a treatment is healing, nothing is drafted for it. You get an alert on your phone right away, without the client’s words in it, and the only reply you can approve is one fixed line asking them to call you.

What gets gated

  • Post-treatment concerns and reactions
  • Symptoms, medications or health history
  • Photos of a treatment area
  • Any request for medical advice

Zero-PHI by design: it never asks for health information, and anything clinical is routed to you instead of into the booking flow. Instagram keeps a copy of every message sent through it, as it does for any DM.

Questions

What med spa owners ask first.

Does it reply on its own?

No. Every reply waits in Telegram until you, or someone you choose, taps Approve. If nobody taps, nothing sends.

What if a draft is wrong?

Tap Don’t send and answer from Instagram yourself. Drafts only use wording you approved at setup, so they can’t invent a price or a promise. Messages it can’t place, like a photo or a one-word DM, get no draft at all: you just get a note to open Instagram.

What happens at night?

A 10 p.m. DM gets a draft in about a minute and a ping on your phone. Approve it from the couch and they have your booking link while they’re still looking. If you’re asleep, the draft waits for your tap in the morning. Nothing sends without you.

What about medical questions?
